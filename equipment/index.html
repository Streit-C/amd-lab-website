Combinatorial Sputtering Chamber Physical Vapor Deposition

The Kurt J. Lesker PVD 200 sputtering chamber uses four independently controlled magnetron guns to fabricate compositionally graded and multicomponent thin-film libraries for rapid exploration of composition-structure-property relationships.

NHT3 Nanoindenter Nanoindentation

The Anton Paar NHT3 nanoindenter quantifies nanoscale mechanical properties, including hardness, elastic modulus, creep, and viscoelastic response, in thin films, coatings, and bulk materials using instrumented indentation measurements.

resource
Flash DSC 2+ Non-Equilibrium Thermal Measurement

The METTLER TOLEDO Flash DSC 2+ measures rapid thermal transitions and transformation kinetics at heating rates up to 50,000 K/s and cooling rates up to 40,000 K/s.

resource
DSC 5+ Equilibrium Thermal Measurement

The METTLER TOLEDO DSC 5+ is an automated differential scanning calorimeter for measuring thermal transitions and reaction behavior from -150 to 700 C, with unattended processing of up to 96 samples and seven reference crucibles.

resource
Benchtop X-ray Florescence Elemental Composition

The Bowman K Series XRF system enables spatially resolved elemental analysis across large samples through programmable X-Y positioning, selectable measurement spot sizes, and simultaneous quantification of up to 30 elements from aluminum through uranium.

Collaborative Robot Sample Transfer

The DOBOT six-axis collaborative robot, equipped with a Gecko gripper, enables programmable and repeatable pick-and-place, sample handling, and materials-transfer workflows, with compliant gripping of flat, smooth, or irregularly shaped objects.
